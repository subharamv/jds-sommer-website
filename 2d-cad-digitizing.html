FAQ · 2D CAD digitising

Questions about 2D CAD digitising.

What to expect when you send us plans, drawings or scans.

Still have a question? Call +49 (0)69 / 905 469-0 or write to info@jds-sommer.de.

Which drawing sizes can you digitise?

Anything from DIN A4 to A0, and also drawings that are a multiple of A0. Yellowed, folded and oversized originals are no problem.

Which CAD formats do you deliver?

AutoCAD DWG or DXF, MicroStation DGN and Nemetschek Allplan FT. Other CAD systems are supplied via DXF, and further formats are available on request.

What is the difference between Basic, Premium and Premium Plus?

DWG-Basic (€49 per A0) covers architecture and engineering drawings with the JDS Basic layer set, checked by our draftsmen. DWG-Premium (€75) adds more fields and Allplan output, up to 56 layers or your own layers, checked by JDS engineers. DWG-Premium Plus (from €99) follows your complete CAD specification and includes extra services such as plausibility checks across several plans. All prices are plus VAT.

Can you follow our own CAD standard?

Yes. You can specify layering, layer colours, drawing scale, text size, and line widths and types. With Premium Plus we also follow your fonts, dimension styles, layout borders, title blocks and plot style tables.

Our drawings are already scanned. Can you work from image files?

Yes. If scans are already available, for example as PDF, TIFF or JPEG, we digitise them into CAD directly without scanning them again.

How fast can you deliver?

For small to medium quantities you can choose from three delivery times: Standard in 5 days, Urgent in 3 days, or Express in only 24 hours. For larger projects we agree the schedule with you.

How do you make sure the CAD files are accurate?

A high rate of automatic vectorisation is followed by on-screen post-processing by CAD experts and quality control by our draftsmen or engineers. The results are 99% equivalent to drawings redrawn from scratch by a qualified CAD expert.

Do I get my original drawings back?

Yes. We return your original drawings free of charge after the project is completed.
