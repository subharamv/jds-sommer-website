Core services

From paper and point clouds
to exact CAD & BIM.

We convert scan data into CAD models in 2D and 3D. They reproduce your plans and objects exactly, with graphics, geometric objects, symbols, text and dimensions, ready for planning, engineering design and building survey.

01

2D CAD Digitization

Exact digitization of large-format technical drawings and as-built plans, from A4 to a multiple of A0. We scan to PDF, TIFF or JPEG for archiving, then vectorize into CAD with dimensions, text, symbols and a layer structure, following your CAD specification for fonts, line types and line weights.

  • AutoCAD DWG / DXF, MicroStation DGN, Allplan
  • Reworked and quality-assured by CAD draftsmen
  • Already scanned? We digitize your image files directly

from €49 plus VAT

Explore 2D CAD
02

3D Modeling for BIM & Components

Precise 3D CAD building models for planners, architects and building owners, created from laser scans and/or 2D as-built plans. We also reverse-engineer machine parts up to 150 cm from our in-house 3D scans or existing engineering drawings.

  • Scan to BIM in Revit (RVT / IFC), Allplan and Archicad (PLN)
  • Level of detail defined by you, LOD 100 to 500
  • Follows your BIM guidelines, Revit families and templates

point cloud to 3D model from €250 plus VAT

Is JDS Sommer operating as usual while the website is redeveloped?

Yes. Only the website is being rebuilt. Projects, quotes and advice continue as usual. Reach us by phone on +49 (0)69 / 905 469-0, by email at info@jds-sommer.de, or with the contact form on this page.

Which services do you offer?

Our two core services are 2D CAD digitization of paper plans and scans, and 3D modeling: Scan to BIM for buildings and industrial structures, and 3D CAD models of machine parts. We also offer scanning, OCR text recognition, GIS digitizing and FM as-built drawings. Pages for these will follow on the new website.

How do I get a price for my project?

Call us or send us your inquiry. We advise you free of charge and prepare an individual offer based on the number and type of your originals. As a guide: 2D CAD digitization starts at €49 per A0 drawing and 3D modeling of a machine part at €250, plus VAT.

Can I test the quality before ordering?

Yes. We create a test scan or test CAD file from a section of your plan, free of charge and without any obligation.

How do I send you drawings or data?

Paper originals can be sent by mail, and we return them free of charge after the project. Scans and digital data can be sent by email. For large volumes, get in touch and we will arrange the best way to transfer your files.

Who are your customers?

We serve approximately 2,000 customers from industry, energy utilities, the public sector, construction and many other sectors, and have done so for 30 years.

Where are you located?
