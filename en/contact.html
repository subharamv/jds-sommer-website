FAQ

Questions, answered.

The essentials about working with JDS Sommer.

Still have a question? We are happy to advise you personally on +49 (0)69 / 905 469-0 or by email at info@jds-sommer.de.

How do I send you my existing data or plans?

Send us digital files, scans or point clouds conveniently by email; for larger volumes we are happy to provide a protected upload link. Simply post paper originals to us; after the project we return them to you free of charge.

How are the costs for my project calculated?

Simply send us a non-binding enquiry with a selection of your originals. We advise you free of charge and prepare an individual fixed-price offer based on scope and level of detail. As a first guide: 2D CAD digitalisation starts at €49 per A0 drawing, 3D modelling at €250 (each plus VAT).

Can I test the CAD quality in advance without obligation?

Yes, of course. We review your source data in advance and, on request, create a sample CAD file from a section of your plan free of charge and without obligation, exactly to your specifications and layer structures.

What delivery times are realistic for a project?

For standard 2D CAD digitalisation we usually deliver within 3 to 5 working days, depending on scope; for urgent projects we also offer express processing by arrangement. For extensive as-built projects and 3D models we agree a binding schedule with you individually in the offer.

Where is JDS Sommer located?

JDS SOMMER GmbH, August-Schanz-Straße 28, D-60433 Frankfurt am Main. We are available Monday to Friday from 8:30 am to 5:00 pm. We are also happy to welcome you in person by appointment.
