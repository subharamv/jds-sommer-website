When scanning objects in 3D, such as buildings or machine parts, it is possible that hidden parts of the object may not be captured or that edges may be blurred. These blurring effects in the generation of the scanned image of a plan or the point cloud of an object can lead to inaccuracies or errors during further processing.

Particularly during the subsequent conversion to CAD data or CAD models, whether in 2D or 3D, the JDS process can lead to inaccuracies in the scan data resulting in errors in the CAD file or model. Depending on the original, object, and field of expertise, the generated CAD data and models can be expected to correspond between 80% and 99% to the input provided by a CAD professional.
