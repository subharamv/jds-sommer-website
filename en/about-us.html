Our story

From paper archives to intelligent digital files.

JDS SOMMER GmbH was founded in 1984 in Frankfurt am Main by its owner and managing director, Dipl.-Ing. Dan Sommer. Then as now, JDS remains committed to its philosophy and the motto “Digital instead of paper.”

From the outset, JDS focused on optimising workflows through fast, direct and secure access to drawings and documents within companies. We digitised and reorganized entire paper archives, from text documents to complex large-format drawings.

The need quickly became apparent to convert these documents into intelligent digital files: exact reproductions of the paper originals, but editable in the respective software. JDS worked intensively on a solution, and the full range of it is described on these pages.

Know-how

Scanning & computer-aided digitalisation.

JDS combines optimal scanning, computer-aided digitalisation and sound engineering expertise to offer cost-effective, fast and high-quality paper-to-CAD and paper-to-text conversion. About 75% of drawings and documents are converted automatically, with dimensions and text largely interpreted correctly. That keeps our prices very competitive.

Quality assurance and technical correction are then done on screen by CAD specialists, engineers and media designers. A core team of 16 manages a growing network of about fifty freelance CAD designers and engineers from all disciplines, from surveyors to energy technicians, as well as OCR and XML specialists.
