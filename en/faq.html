    Popular articles
    Is JDS Sommer operating as usual while the website is redeveloped?

    Yes. Only the website is being rebuilt. Projects, quotes and advice continue as usual. Reach us by phone on +49 (0)69 / 905 469-0, by email at info@jds-sommer.de, or with the contact form on this page.

    Which services do you offer?

    Six service areas, as in our Services menu: 2D CAD digitalisation, 3D and BIM modelling, modelling by discipline (architecture, structure and building services), as-built and facility documentation, GIS and infrastructure data, and drawing and document digitisation. Not sure which one fits? Send us your plans and we will advise you free of charge.

    How do I get a price for my project?

    Call us or send us your enquiry. We advise you free of charge and prepare an individual offer based on the number and type of your originals. As a guide: 2D CAD digitalisation starts at €49 per A0 drawing and 3D modelling of a machine part at €250, plus VAT.

    Can I test the quality before ordering?

    Yes. We audit a sample of your drawings or data and can create a sample CAD file from a section of your plan, free of charge and without any obligation.

    Which file formats do you deliver?

    2D drawings are delivered as AutoCAD DWG/DXF, MicroStation DGN or Nemetschek Allplan files. 3D and BIM models are delivered in Revit (RVT/IFC), Allplan, Archicad, IGES or STEP. If you need another format, just ask.

    How quickly can you deliver?

    For 2D CAD digitalisation you can choose delivery in 5 days, 3 days or 24 hours. For larger volumes and 3D projects we agree a schedule with you in the offer.

    How do I send you drawings or data?

    Original drawings can be sent by mail, and we return them free of charge after the project. Scans and digital data can be sent by email. For a defined project, get in touch to agree the best way to transfer your files.

    Who are your customers?

    We serve approximately 2,000 customers from industry, energy utilities, the public sector, construction and many other sectors, and have done so for 42 years.

    Where are you located?

    JDS SOMMER GmbH, August-Schanz-Straße 28, D-60433 Frankfurt am Main. We are available Monday to Friday, 8:30 am to 5:00 pm, and you are welcome to visit us in person.

    What is drawing-to-CAD conversion?

    We convert paper drawings, PDFs and scanned plans into precise, editable 2D CAD files, keeping the geometry, dimensions, text, symbols and layer structure you need. You receive AutoCAD DWG, DXF, MicroStation DGN or, where agreed, Allplan files.

    Can you create 2D drawings from a point cloud?

    Yes. With Scan-to-CAD we turn registered point clouds and laser-scan data into 2D floor plans, sections and elevations of the existing building, with walls, openings and levels as agreed. Existing plans can be used for reference and comparison.

    Can you bring our existing CAD files into line with our CAD standard?

    Yes. With CAD restructuring and standardisation we align existing DWG, DXF or DGN files with your CAD guidelines: layer standards and naming, line styles, fonts, dimensions and drawing conventions.

    What is the difference between Basic, Premium and Premium Plus?

    DWG-Basic (€49 per A0) covers architecture and engineering drawings with the JDS Basic layer set, checked by our draftsmen. DWG-Premium (€75) adds more fields and Allplan output, up to 56 layers or your own layers, checked by JDS engineers. DWG-Premium Plus (from €99) follows your complete CAD specification and includes extra services such as plausibility checks across several plans. All prices are plus VAT.

    Which drawing sizes can you digitise?

    Anything from DIN A4 to A0, and also drawings that are a multiple of A0. Yellowed, folded and oversized originals are no problem.

    How do you make sure the CAD files are accurate?

    Software-assisted CAD conversion is followed by on-screen post-processing by CAD experts and quality control by our draftsmen or engineers. The results are 99% equivalent to drawings redrawn from scratch by a qualified CAD expert.

    What is Scan-to-BIM?

    We turn registered point clouds and laser-scan data into detailed 3D BIM models of the captured building or facility, based on the agreed modelling requirements. You receive the model in Revit (RVT), IFC, Allplan or Archicad.

    Can you create a BIM model from our 2D plans?

    Yes. With CAD-to-BIM modelling we turn DWG, DXF and DGN drawings, floor plans, sections and elevations into structured 3D BIM models with walls, slabs, doors and windows as agreed. Existing PDFs and scanned plans can also be used, subject to suitability.

    What is as-built BIM modelling?

    A BIM model of a building or facility as it exists today, created from point clouds, laser scans, existing drawings and survey information. It is the basis for later planning and documentation.

    Do you scan buildings on site?

    For buildings and industrial structures we work with surveying and engineering offices that carry out the on-site 3D laser scanning. They send us the point clouds, and we deliver the editable 3D CAD or BIM model. Machine parts and objects up to 150 cm we scan in-house.

    Which level of detail (LOD) can you model?

    Anything from LOD 100 to LOD 500. You define the level of detail and with it the accuracy of your model, including components such as walls, ceilings, stairs, windows and doors with their properties.

    Can you model machine parts in 3D CAD?

    Yes. We create 3D CAD models of components from 3D scan data, STL meshes, engineering drawings or the physical part, delivered as STEP or IGES, or in SolidEdge or Autodesk Inventor. Modelling a part up to 40 cm edge length starts at €250 plus VAT.

    Can you model industrial facilities and plants?

    Yes. We create 3D CAD and BIM models of industrial facilities and technical installations from point clouds, laser scans and existing plans, including equipment and piping within the agreed scope.

    Which disciplines can you model?

    Architecture, structure, electrical, HVAC and plumbing, and plant. Each discipline can be its own model or combined in one coordinated model, in native Revit, Allplan or Archicad and following your BIM guidelines.

    What does an MEP model include?

    For electrical engineering: power supply, lighting, cable trays and security systems. For HVAC and plumbing: ductwork networks, air outlets and piping systems, modelled as-built.

    What does a structural model include?

    Load-bearing walls, columns, beams, slabs and foundations, as a structural model that can be used on its own or combined with the architectural model.

    Can you work with our BIM guidelines and Revit families?

    Yes. We follow your specifications and BIM guidelines, including your Revit families and templates for Revit, Allplan or Archicad.

    What are as-built drawings?

    Drawings that document the actual state of a building, including all changes made during construction and later conversions. We create them from construction and permit drawings, marked-up plans, point clouds or site measurements, as floor plans, sections and elevations to your CAD standard.

    Can you prepare plans for our CAFM system?

    Yes. We create floor plans with room polygons, areas, room numbers and use as attributes, structured to your CAFM layer and attribute rules so they are ready for import. Delivery as DWG, DXF or IFC.

    Can you create plans from survey data?

    Yes. We turn total station and GNSS data, registered point clouds and hand sketches with measurements into to-scale floor plans, sections and elevations, with levels and heights from the survey.

    Can you update our existing CAD drawings after a conversion?

    Yes. We update your existing CAD drawings from site measurements, sketches or photos, keep your layers, blocks and standard, and mark or document the changes.

    Can you convert utility maps into GIS data?

    Yes. We digitise water, gas, power and telecom network maps, with lines, fittings and house connections as objects and material, diameter and year as attributes. You receive the data for GIS or CAD, for example as Shapefile, DXF, DWG or DGN.

    Can you convert our CAD drawings into GIS data?

    Yes. We turn CAD drawings from any system into topologically clean GIS data: closed areas, connected lines and attributes taken from text and blocks, in the right coordinate system and your target data model.

    Can you georeference and update old maps?

    Yes. We georeference scanned maps and CAD plans using control points and survey data, and bring historical maps of roads, rail, sewers and supply networks to current coordinate reference systems. Existing map data can also be updated with new construction and changes.

    Can you scan large-format drawings?

    Yes, from A4 up to a multiple of A0, including folded, yellowed or fragile plans, transparent originals and rolls. You receive high-resolution scans as TIFF, PDF or JPEG, named to your scheme, and your originals come back free of charge.

    How do you convert paper and PDF drawings into CAD?

    CAD specialists convert your paper or PDF drawings into editable CAD drawings on screen, and engineers check every drawing before delivery. Drawing structure and technical information are preserved according to the agreed scope.

    Can you digitise drawings and plans for a defined project?

    Yes. We agree the scope first, then scan, index or convert the selected drawings and documents to match your file structure. CAD conversion and searchable PDFs are available where needed.
