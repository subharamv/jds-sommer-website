Industries · 08

Large-format plans for stations, bridges and tracks.

Stations, bridges and track facilities: large-format plans up to a multiple of A0, converted into precise CAD drawings for maintenance, inspection and expansion.

Start enquiry All industries

The challenge

Long assets, long drawings.

Infrastructure is built to last for generations. Its documentation consists of very long and large-format drawings, structural calculations and survey plans that have to remain usable for inspections and renewals.

We scan drawings up to a multiple of A0 and convert them to scale into CAD, with correct dimensions and text, and model structures such as bridges and station buildings in 3D when a project needs it.

What we typically receive

  • Track and route plans
  • Bridge and structure drawings
  • Station floor plans
  • Survey and site plans

Delivered as

  • MicroStation DGN
  • AutoCAD DWG
  • IFC

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Common questions from customers in this industry.

Still have a question? Call +49 (0)69 / 905 469-0 or write to info@jds-sommer.de.

Which formats do you deliver for infrastructure projects?

MicroStation DGN and AutoCAD DWG for drawings, and IFC for 3D models of bridges and station buildings. Other formats are available on request.

Which drawing sizes can you digitise?

Anything from DIN A4 to A0, and also drawings that are a multiple of A0. Yellowed, folded and oversized originals are no problem.

Can you scan large-format drawings?

Yes, from A4 up to a multiple of A0, including folded, yellowed or fragile plans, transparent originals and rolls. You receive high-resolution scans as TIFF, PDF or JPEG, named to your scheme, and your originals come back free of charge.

What does a structural model include?

Load-bearing walls, columns, beams, slabs and foundations, as a structural model that can be used on its own or combined with the architectural model.

Can I test the quality before ordering?

Yes. We audit a sample of your drawings or data and can create a sample CAD file from a section of your plan, free of charge and without any obligation.
